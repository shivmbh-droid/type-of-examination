TYPES OF EXAMINATION

The different types of examinations are important for the following reasons:

  • Evaluation of knowledge: They help measure students' understanding of different subjects.
  • Identification of weaknesses: They reveal topics in which students need additional practice.
  • Regular learning: They encourage students to study and revise lessons throughout the year.
  • Development of skills: They improve reasoning, problem-solving, communication, and practical abilities.
  • Feedback and improvement: Examination results help teachers guide students and improve teaching methods.
  • Educational opportunities: Some examinations help students gain admission to higher education or qualify for scholarships and careers.
  • Self-confidence: Preparation and practice can help students become more confident in their abilities.
  • Time management: Examinations teach students to organise their time and complete tasks within a given period.
